Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

RaceRecap (“the App”) is provided by [Joel Stolk]. You can reach us at RaceRecap.app@gmail.com.

1. What the App does

RaceRecap lets you enter race results and create shareable result cards.

2. Data stored on your device

The race results, names, times, photos and settings you enter are stored on your device. We do not run a server that receives this data, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising or tracking. The App does not require an account.

3. Optional result scan (AI processing)

If you choose to scan your results, the images you select are sent to a third-party AI service that reads the times and names in them. This only happens after you give permission in the App, and only for the images you pick. The service returns the extracted data to the App.

[Add how long the AI service keeps the images, once you have confirmed it with Rork or the provider. For example: “The images are processed to produce the result and are not kept afterwards.” Only write this if it is true.]

You can use the App without this feature by entering your results manually.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos of results or for your cards.
  • Photo library: to let you pick images, and to save finished cards to your photos when you tap Save.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

5. Sharing

Cards are only shared when you choose to share them, through your device’s share sheet.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your rights and deleting your data

Because your data is stored on your device, you can delete it by clearing the data in the App or by uninstalling the App. For questions or requests about your data, email RaceRecap.app@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Support

Need help with RaceRecap, found a bug, or have an idea for the App? Email RaceRecap.app@gmail.com.

To help us solve your problem faster, include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

RaceRecap is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any race organizer.
